# ADR-0004: Codec2 at 3200 bps as the vocoder

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-21 (recorded; decision predates the repository)
**Phase:** Pre-development

## Context

Hard requirement 7 is audio no worse than DMR. DMR uses AMBE+2 at 2450 bps, which carries per-unit
royalties and is only available as licensed IP or in a hardware vocoder chip.

The alternative is Codec2 — open source, no royalties, and proven to run on an STM32F4 by the M17
project, which is the closest existing precedent for this kind of build.

## Decision

Codec2 at 3200 bps, with ~47% FEC and framing overhead, giving ~4.7 kbps on the wire — the same
overhead ratio DMR applies to AMBE+2.

Codec2 3200 has been listened to against AMBE 2400 samples and judged acceptable.

## Consequences

- No royalty per handset, and no vocoder chip on the BOM. Material at production volume, and it
  removes a supply-chain dependency of the kind that eliminated the IWAVE Defensor-T4.
- Runs on the STM32F4 already selected — no separate DSP.
- 3200 bps is the *most expensive* Codec2 mode, and it sets the slot budget. It is the reason the
  channel is 25 kHz rather than 12.5 kHz, and it is one side of the arithmetic in
  [ADR-0002](0002-tdma-slot-pipelining.md) that currently does not close.
- Codec2 has lower modes — 2400, 1600, 1300 — each a step down in quality. If
  [OQ-0002](../open-questions.md) resolves against the current budget, dropping to Codec2 2400
  is the option that most directly relieves it, at the cost of the "no worse than DMR" requirement
  becoming a much closer call. Prefer the other three escapes first.
- 47% is inherited from DMR's ratio, not derived from this channel's error characteristics. The
  right FEC strength depends on the 4FSK BER curve at the working SNR, which is unmeasured. It may
  legitimately be lower — see [OQ-0002](../open-questions.md).

## Reversal trigger

Bench listening in Phase 1, over the real FEC and real channel errors rather than clean samples,
fails the "no worse than DMR" bar.

## Alternatives rejected

- **AMBE+2.** Royalties, licensing, and a hardware dependency. Would otherwise be the safe choice.
- **Opus.** Excellent, but not designed for this bit rate over a channel with no retransmission, and
  its framing assumptions do not suit a fixed slotted MAC.
- **Codec2 1300 / 1600.** Would make the slot budget trivially comfortable and fail requirement 7.
